The problem

A status is not care.

Today

Rajesh, 58, has a positive stool test. He is referred for a colonoscopy. Weeks later the referral shows COMPLETED. No colonoscopy was done and no report exists. Nobody notices until he is diagnosed later.

With Tasleem

The same referral says COMPLETED, but there is no procedure and no report. Tasleem flags it, names Dr. Fatima as owner, escalates when she is busy, and will not let the case close until the report arrives.

In published programmes, 30–55% of people with an abnormal stool test complete a colonoscopy in safety-net settings, against 70–80% in organised programmes with tracking, a named owner and navigation. BMJ Open, 2026

How it works

Four steps. One accountable loop.

01

Sense

Reads referral, booking and result events from Malaffi and labs (HL7 v2, FHIR R4).

02

Decide

Fixed, tested rules spot stalls: booked but not attended, completed without a report, referral rejected, no reply.

03

Act

Assigns one owner, escalates on missed deadlines, and reaches each person on their own channel: Sahatna for patients, the EMR inbox for clinicians.

04

Hand off

A clinician closes every case, on evidence. The AI assistant drafts; a person always decides.

Safety

Clinical teams stay in charge.

R1

Every open case has one named owner.

R2

A handoff counts only when the receiver accepts.

R3

A case closes only on evidence or a signed clinician decision.

R4

Every missed deadline reaches a named person.

R5

AI suggests. It never closes a case, sets priority or advises a patient.

R6

Every change is written to a tamper-evident audit log.

Patient messages never contain results. Abnormal findings are discussed in person, as the IFHAS standard requires.

Built on what exists

Malaffi referral events (Circular 210/2026) Sahatna patient app HL7 v2 and FHIR R4 SNOMED CT and LOINC Arabic and English

Tasleem is a layer beside Malaffi and Sahatna. It doesn't replace them.
